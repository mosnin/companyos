# Operator Command Center — Program v5 Contract

## North star

A self-improving company control plane that turns ambitious direction into
independently verified outcomes.

## Current outcome

Give a Company OS operator one exceptional decision surface for direction,
stage, change, agents, evidence, quality, cost, authority, and the exact next
decision.

## Acceptance outcome

An operator can open one read-only command center in Markdown, JSON, or
self-contained HTML and, without reading raw state or every skill, correctly
answer:

1. What changed in governed reality?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What outcome and stage are current?
4. What is the single next decision or action?
5. Who owns it, what must they produce, and how is it verified?
6. Which quality exceptions block advancement?
7. What are managers and agent runs actually doing, with what model and budget
   evidence?
8. What evidence, feedback, authority, schedule, and cancellation posture can
   be trusted?

## Hard quality gate

- All 13 experience dimensions score at least 9.0/10 in an independent audit.
- Every adversarial acceptance-matrix case passes.
- The complete controller, transactional-store, observation, reference,
  distribution, compile, and installation suites pass.
- The projection is read-only and deterministic.
- No signed token, nonce, private key, raw provider envelope, or arbitrary
  control-state field appears in any output format.
- Requested model identity is never represented as provider-observed identity.
- Missing or invalid telemetry is never converted to zero.
- No release version is bumped or installed before independent acceptance.

## Product sequence

1. Reality: bind the rejected first audit and self-hosting defects.
2. Intelligence: bind the product thesis, current primary sources, and
   counterevidence.
3. Direction: commit this exact experience contract and one primary capability.
4. Experience: implement and independently audit the real rendered journey.
5. Delivery: version, test, and package only the accepted candidate.
6. Verification: verify exact install parity and self-host the installed build.
7. Learning: record measured operator outcomes and bounded adaptations.

## Frozen boundaries

- No Chippy edit or integration.
- No provider launch, production environment, or customer-data mutation.
- No recurring scheduler enablement.
- No hosted or writable web dashboard, team-room concept, or unrelated feature
  expansion; the HTML surface remains a self-contained read-only projection.
- No weakening of transactional authority, external issuer separation, phase
  gates, project isolation, or independent review.
