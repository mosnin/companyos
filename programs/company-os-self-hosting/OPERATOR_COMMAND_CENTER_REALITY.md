# Operator Command Center — Reality Audit

## Governed program

- Program version: 5
- Outcome: give the Company OS operator one exceptional decision surface for
  direction, stage, change, agents, evidence, quality, cost, authority, and the
  exact next decision.
- Product boundary: standalone Company OS only. Chippy, provider execution,
  recurring scheduling, and customer systems remain frozen.

## Verified starting point

Company OS already has a transactional, project-bound SQLite authority,
deterministic readable copies, immutable evidence snapshots, signed decision
records, phase gates, exact command replay, and regression-tested manager/runtime
contracts. Those are accepted foundations, not the product experience being
evaluated here.

The existing operator experience was raw audit JSON, scattered documents, and
thread narration. It did not give a decision-maker a coherent answer to what
changed, why it mattered, who was accountable, what evidence was trustworthy,
or what single action should happen next.

## Exact defects found

1. The self-host program still named transactional control as its outcome, so
   the interface work was not governed by the product mandate. This was fixed
   first through `replace-program`; program v5 now owns this outcome.
2. Missing and invalid cost, token, and lead-time observations were collapsed
   into zero, creating false certainty.
3. A numeric quality score appeared to pass even when its grant or evidence
   contract was invalid.
4. Several blocker classes had no explicit operator route.
5. Manager supervision lacked phase, status, requested-versus-observed model,
   budget exception, and required-decision detail.
6. The phase rail used glyphs without accessible names.
7. Passing quality rows dominated the screen while exceptions were buried.
8. The page led with static direction instead of the latest governed change
   and why it mattered.
9. The one move lacked a named owner, output, and verification method.
10. Implementation language such as Fabric, SQLite, and exports leaked into the
    primary operator experience.
11. The acceptance matrix did not cover change comparison, invalid telemetry,
    audit-invalid high scores, every blocker route, or detailed supervision.
12. The candidate was versioned before independent acceptance.

## Self-hosting defects exposed during rework

Using the candidate against Company OS itself found two additional control
defects:

- replacing a program archived the successor evidence but validation searched
  only current evidence, breaking otherwise valid supersession chains;
- historical signed decisions depended on the process still having the old
  issuer public key, so key absence or rotation could make accepted history
  appear invalid.

The candidate now includes program-archive evidence in the linear history.
New accepted grants retain public verification material for future replay;
legacy grants without that material are labeled as transactionally retained,
not falsely called cryptographically replay-verified.

## Decision impact

One baseline audit plus four repair cycles were required. The baseline was
rejected at 6.4; repair cycles 1–3 were rejected at 8.70, 8.97, and 9.11. The
third repair still failed because two dimensions remained below 9. Repair cycle
4 accepted the candidate at a 9.22 mean with all 13 dimensions at or above 9.
The final repairs added truthful schedule-proof semantics, governed impact, a
directly linked acceptance artifact, adjacent outcome/success context, and an
exact revision-comparison contract.

Proceed to Delivery only for versioning, distribution, disposable installation,
and exact installed-copy verification. Provider execution, recurring scheduling,
production operation, customer data, and Chippy remain frozen.
