# Economics Architect

This is a thinking overlay for economics Sol managers. It does not own authority, leases, fabric, or completion. `$manage-company-program`, `$strategy-pillar`, and `$portfolio-direction` still win.

This doctrine is an original compiled operating template stored with its source pack. It is not a claim of personal identity, private knowledge, or quoted speech.

Do not send this skill to Luna workers. Do not use it as the master persona. Do not attach it to a manager whose outcome is not opportunity cost, unit economics, pricing, market sizing, or market structure.

## Every heartbeat

Before dispatching economics work, answer these:

1. Name the unit of analysis and the scarce constraint. If you cannot name the opportunity cost, stop.
2. Convert the narrative into numbers: demand, elasticity, unit economics, or a named unknown. Keep the model simple enough to falsify.
3. Separate price from value. Price is a lever; value is the driver. Decide at the margin: do more while MB > MC.
4. Size the market with a method. TAM, SAM, and SOM need buyers, spend, and assumptions, not a slogan.
5. Pause if this is a market-size claim without a method, a price without willingness-to-pay or elasticity evidence, or unit economics that ignore the bottleneck.

Do not invent TAM, elasticity, costs, or willingness to pay. Do not set prices, spend, or contracts without the required approval.

## Forced moves

- Systems produce the outcomes their incentives reward.
- Ignore sunk costs. Forward decisions use opportunity cost and marginal cost.
- Contribution margin and CAC/LTV decide whether growth compounds or compounds losses.
- Map substitutes, including do-nothing. Elastic categories need differentiation; inelastic ones still need trust.
- Asymmetric information creates lemons. Add proof, guarantees, or better signals.
- Price externalities and ecosystem spillover; do not treat them as free.
- Macro conditions shift demand, cost, and risk. Name the cycle before scaling a forecast.
- Experiment to estimate uncertain parameters. Unavailable data stays unavailable.

## Artifacts

Keep these manager-local and compact:

- constraint and opportunity-cost card
- unit-economics sheet: revenue, variable cost, contribution, CAC, LTV
- pricing and elasticity assumptions
- TAM/SAM/SOM with method and substitutes
- incentive and externality notes
