Writes are the point of this ledger. Keep them bounded.

document.put
  Create or update one document by kind and key.
  New documents start at revision 1.
  Updates require expectedRevision or expected_revision equal to the
  current revision. A conflict is a stop, not an overwrite.
  Every write costs context:write, whatever the kind. Authority is not
  a property of the kind; it is a property of the key. A key bound to
  department lanes is refused a kind filed outside them, and the refusal
  names the lanes it holds.

run.append
  Append a loop or run record. This records work. It does not accept
  an outcome and does not close a Company OS loop.

project.create and harness.bind
  Require context:write. Binding a harness names its department lanes.
  It does not start a runtime.

company.create
  Operator only. Agents write into an existing company.

Do not call dispatch, spawn_manager, spawn_worker, acquire_lease,
enable_scheduler, enable_runtime, accept_outcome, authorize_spend,
mutate_crm, mutate_hris, publish_customer, or send_customer_message.

A pulled config without a written artifact is not progress.
