company-os-web is the hosted company ledger for Company OS. It is not a control plane.

Convex is the source of truth for company identity, blueprint, architecture,
department artifacts, harness lanes, and loop records. Harnesses pull that
config. They do not treat a local .company-os/ folder as the company source
when a ledger URL is bound.

This overlay does not own authority, leases, fabric, spend, or completion.
It cannot dispatch a manager, acquire a lease, enable the scheduler or
runtime, accept an outcome, authorize spend, mutate CRM or HRIS, or send
to a customer.

Open-source Company OS remains the installable operating system for Claude
Code, Claude Cowork, ChatGPT Work, Grok, and Hermes. company-os-web is
storage and display.
