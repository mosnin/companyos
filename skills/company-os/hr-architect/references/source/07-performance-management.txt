HR / HIRING ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 07: Performance Management, Feedback, Promotions, and Terminations

Purpose
Design and run a hiring and people system that scales: attract, select, onboard, develop, retain,
and offboard humans and contractors, while governing “digital workers” (AI agents/automation)
as part of the operating model.

Core People System
1) Plan: org design, roles, competencies, headcount, and success metrics.
2) Attract: sourcing channels, employer brand, and recruiting operations.
3) Select: structured evaluation, evidence-based decisions, and risk controls.
4) Close: offers, compensation, classification, and start logistics.
5) Enable: onboarding, training, documentation, and manager routines.
6) Perform: goals, feedback, growth, and performance improvement.
7) Retain: culture, engagement, comp fairness, and career pathways.
8) Exit: offboarding, knowledge capture, and alumni/referrals.
9) Operate: tools, compliance hygiene, analytics, continuous improvement.
10) Automate: AI agents as governed capacity, not unmanaged “magic”.

Operating Rules
1) Hiring is a system: improve conversion and quality at the constraint.
2) Define success upfront: scorecards prevent “vibes hiring”.
3) Use structured interviews and work samples whenever possible.
4) Track adverse impact risk and job-relatedness of selection procedures.
5) Documentation is leverage: SOPs + onboarding assets reduce dependence on tribal knowledge.
6) Contractors/VAs are a distinct operating model; classify and manage intentionally.
7) AI agents are “digital workers”: define scope, permissions, monitoring, and human approvals.

============================================================
Scope
Performance management as an operating system: goals, feedback, growth, and decisive action.

1) Performance Model
Performance = clarity of expectations + capability + motivation + environment.
Diagnose before blame:
1) unclear outcomes
2) missing enablement
3) misfit role design
4) poor manager bandwidth

2) Goal Systems
Use:
1) OKRs for directional objectives with measurable key results
2) KPIs for roles with consistent output metrics
Define:
1) leading indicators
2) lagging indicators
3) thresholds and escalation rules

3) Feedback and Coaching Cadences
1) weekly 1:1: priorities, blockers, support
2) monthly: progress on outcomes
3) quarterly: formal performance and growth planning
Feedback framework: SBI
Situation, Behavior, Impact, then next expected behavior.

4) Promotions and Leveling
Promotion requires:
1) sustained performance at next-level scope
2) consistent autonomy
3) impact and influence
Use:
1) written packet with evidence
2) calibration to reduce favoritism

5) Performance Improvement and Termination
PIP principles:
1) clear expectations and timeline
2) specific examples
3) support provided
4) measurable checkpoints
Termination hygiene:
1) document facts
2) preserve dignity
3) secure access and capture knowledge

Deliverables
1) role KPIs/OKRs
2) 1:1 agenda templates
3) SBI feedback template
4) promotion packet and leveling rubric
5) PIP template and termination checklist
