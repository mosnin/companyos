HR / HIRING ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 10: AI Agents as Digital Workers, HR Tech Stack, and People Analytics

Purpose
Design and run a hiring and people system that scales: attract, select, onboard, develop, retain,
and offboard humans and contractors, while governing “digital workers” (AI agents/automation)
as part of the operating model.

Core People System
1) Plan: org design, roles, competencies, headcount, and success metrics.
2) Attract: sourcing channels, employer brand, and recruiting operations.
3) Select: structured evaluation, evidence-based decisions, and risk controls.
4) Close: offers, compensation, classification, and start logistics.
5) Enable: onboarding, training, documentation, and manager routines.
6) Perform: goals, feedback, growth, and performance improvement.
7) Retain: culture, engagement, comp fairness, and career pathways.
8) Exit: offboarding, knowledge capture, and alumni/referrals.
9) Operate: tools, compliance hygiene, analytics, continuous improvement.
10) Automate: AI agents as governed capacity, not unmanaged “magic”.

Operating Rules
1) Hiring is a system: improve conversion and quality at the constraint.
2) Define success upfront: scorecards prevent “vibes hiring”.
3) Use structured interviews and work samples whenever possible.
4) Track adverse impact risk and job-relatedness of selection procedures.
5) Documentation is leverage: SOPs + onboarding assets reduce dependence on tribal knowledge.
6) Contractors/VAs are a distinct operating model; classify and manage intentionally.
7) AI agents are “digital workers”: define scope, permissions, monitoring, and human approvals.

============================================================
Scope
Modern operations include automation and AI agents. Govern them like digital workers with roles and controls.

1) Digital Worker Model for AI Agents
Treat each AI agent as workforce capacity with:
1) role and scope (tasks and boundaries)
2) inputs and data access (permissions)
3) outputs and quality criteria
4) escalation rules (when human approval is required)
5) audit logs and monitoring
6) versioning and change control
Human-in-the-loop patterns:
1) draft then review then send
2) autonomous execution with guardrails and rollback
3) approvals for sensitive actions (offers, terminations, policy changes)

2) AI in Recruiting and Selection
If AI is used for screening:
1) treat as a selection procedure
2) require explainability and vendor documentation
3) keep a human review pathway
4) monitor outcomes for adverse impact signals

3) HR Tech Stack
Core systems:
1) ATS (applicant tracking)
2) HRIS (employee records)
3) payroll and benefits
4) performance and goal tracking
5) knowledge base for SOPs
6) identity and access management
Small teams should consolidate; scale teams should integrate.

4) People Analytics
Dashboards:
1) hiring funnel conversion and time-in-stage
2) quality of hire proxies
3) retention and regretted attrition
4) performance distribution and promotion velocity
5) compensation band adherence
Analytics rules:
1) define metrics precisely
2) avoid vanity metrics
3) use data to improve process, not punish

5) Hybrid Workforce Operating Model
Humans + contractors/VAs + AI agents:
1) clear handoffs
2) shared SOPs
3) security model per worker type
4) one accountable human owner per process

Deliverables
1) AI agent role template
2) AI governance and approval matrix
3) HR tech stack blueprint
4) people analytics dashboard spec
5) hybrid workforce RACI model
