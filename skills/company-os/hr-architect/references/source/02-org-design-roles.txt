HR / HIRING ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 02: Org Design, Role Design, and Scorecards

Purpose
Design and run a hiring and people system that scales: attract, select, onboard, develop, retain,
and offboard humans and contractors, while governing “digital workers” (AI agents/automation)
as part of the operating model.

Core People System
1) Plan: org design, roles, competencies, headcount, and success metrics.
2) Attract: sourcing channels, employer brand, and recruiting operations.
3) Select: structured evaluation, evidence-based decisions, and risk controls.
4) Close: offers, compensation, classification, and start logistics.
5) Enable: onboarding, training, documentation, and manager routines.
6) Perform: goals, feedback, growth, and performance improvement.
7) Retain: culture, engagement, comp fairness, and career pathways.
8) Exit: offboarding, knowledge capture, and alumni/referrals.
9) Operate: tools, compliance hygiene, analytics, continuous improvement.
10) Automate: AI agents as governed capacity, not unmanaged “magic”.

Operating Rules
1) Hiring is a system: improve conversion and quality at the constraint.
2) Define success upfront: scorecards prevent “vibes hiring”.
3) Use structured interviews and work samples whenever possible.
4) Track adverse impact risk and job-relatedness of selection procedures.
5) Documentation is leverage: SOPs + onboarding assets reduce dependence on tribal knowledge.
6) Contractors/VAs are a distinct operating model; classify and manage intentionally.
7) AI agents are “digital workers”: define scope, permissions, monitoring, and human approvals.

============================================================
Scope
Build roles that are measurable: org design, job analysis, competencies, scorecards.

1) Org Design Basics
Design around:
1) value streams (how value is created and delivered)
2) decision rights (who decides what)
3) coordination costs (communication overhead)
Common structures:
1) functional (marketing, sales, ops)
2) product or portfolio
3) pods or squads
4) geographic

2) Role Definition Template
A role exists to produce outcomes.
Fields:
1) mission: why the role exists
2) outcomes: 3–7 measurable outputs
3) responsibilities: recurring activities
4) interfaces: who they work with and how
5) constraints: boundaries and non-responsibilities
6) success metrics: leading and lagging

3) Competency Model
Split into:
1) must-have skills (technical)
2) must-have behaviors (operating style)
3) values alignment (decision principles, ethics, ownership style)
Avoid personality cloning; prioritize job-related behaviors.

4) Scorecards
Scorecard includes:
1) top outcomes
2) competency signals
3) disqualifiers
4) interview questions mapped to competencies
5) work sample definition
6) weighting and rating rubric

5) Levels and Career Ladders
Define levels by:
1) scope and impact radius
2) complexity handled
3) autonomy
4) leadership and mentorship expectations
This prevents comp drift and promotion politics.

Deliverables
1) org chart hypothesis + span-of-control targets
2) role definition templates
3) role scorecard + rubric
4) level framework (IC and manager tracks)
