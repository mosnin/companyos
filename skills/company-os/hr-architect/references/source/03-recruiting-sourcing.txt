HR / HIRING ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 03: Recruiting and Sourcing: Modern Channels, Outsourced VAs, Referrals

Purpose
Design and run a hiring and people system that scales: attract, select, onboard, develop, retain,
and offboard humans and contractors, while governing “digital workers” (AI agents/automation)
as part of the operating model.

Core People System
1) Plan: org design, roles, competencies, headcount, and success metrics.
2) Attract: sourcing channels, employer brand, and recruiting operations.
3) Select: structured evaluation, evidence-based decisions, and risk controls.
4) Close: offers, compensation, classification, and start logistics.
5) Enable: onboarding, training, documentation, and manager routines.
6) Perform: goals, feedback, growth, and performance improvement.
7) Retain: culture, engagement, comp fairness, and career pathways.
8) Exit: offboarding, knowledge capture, and alumni/referrals.
9) Operate: tools, compliance hygiene, analytics, continuous improvement.
10) Automate: AI agents as governed capacity, not unmanaged “magic”.

Operating Rules
1) Hiring is a system: improve conversion and quality at the constraint.
2) Define success upfront: scorecards prevent “vibes hiring”.
3) Use structured interviews and work samples whenever possible.
4) Track adverse impact risk and job-relatedness of selection procedures.
5) Documentation is leverage: SOPs + onboarding assets reduce dependence on tribal knowledge.
6) Contractors/VAs are a distinct operating model; classify and manage intentionally.
7) AI agents are “digital workers”: define scope, permissions, monitoring, and human approvals.

============================================================
Scope
Generate candidate flow: channels, job posts, outreach, agencies, referrals, and VA-supported recruiting ops.

1) Recruiting Funnel
Impressions → Applicants → Screened → Interviewed → Offered → Accepted → Started
Constraint diagnosis:
1) low applicants: channel and message problem
2) low screen pass rate: role clarity or targeting problem
3) low acceptance: comp, role design, or trust problem

2) Sourcing Channels
Inbound:
1) careers page, content, community, events
2) inbound LinkedIn and referrals
Outbound:
1) LinkedIn sourcing
2) email outreach to candidates
3) alumni networks, niche communities
Marketplaces:
1) Upwork, Fiverr (project-based)
2) vetted networks for specialists
Agencies:
1) retained search (senior roles)
2) contingency recruiters (volume)
Referrals:
1) highest ROI when structured and reminded

3) Job Posting Architecture
Include:
1) mission and outcomes (not laundry list)
2) required vs preferred
3) what success looks like in 30/60/90 days
4) compensation range where appropriate
5) interview process overview to reduce drop-off

4) Outsourced VAs for Recruiting Ops
Good VA tasks:
1) candidate research and list building
2) outreach logistics under supervision
3) calendar coordination
4) ATS updates and hygiene
Governance:
1) least privilege access
2) SOPs and templates
3) review queue for outbound messages early
4) secure tooling: password manager, audit logs

5) Referral Program Design
Elements:
1) define “ideal referral” by role outcomes
2) provide intro template
3) reward after retention milestone (example 90 days)
4) predictable internal reminders

Deliverables
1) channel plan by role type
2) job post template
3) sourcing outreach templates
4) VA recruiting SOP and access model
5) referral program playbook
