
PROJECT SCOPING TEMPLATE & DEFINITIONS DOCUMENT

Purpose:
Provide a consistent reference for defining project scopes.

Core Elements:
- Purpose: The reason the project exists.
- Objectives: Specific measurable outcomes.
- Constraints: Limits on scope, resources, budget, timeline.
- Resources: People, tools, systems, budget available.
- Stakeholders: Individuals or groups affected or involved.
- Success Criteria: How completion and quality are measured.
- Deliverables: Tangible outputs created by the project.
- Dependencies: Conditions required before work can progress.
- High-Level Architecture: Structural overview of the project.

Scope Completeness Checklist:
- Does the scope define what is included?
- Does it clarify what is excluded?
- Are deliverables explicit and measurable?
- Are timelines and constraints known?
- Are stakeholders clearly identified?

Examples:
High-quality scopes define boundaries, objectives, measurable criteria, and assumptions explicitly.
