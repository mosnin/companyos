# Compile Outcome Benchmarks

References are evaluation evidence, not inspiration notes.

Each required quality dimension must have bound references with provenance and at least one positive
anchor. When a dimension is subjective, include materially different quality tiers so evaluator
calibration can test whether the judge actually distinguishes weak from strong work.
