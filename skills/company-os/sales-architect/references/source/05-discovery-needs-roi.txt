SALES ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 05: Discovery Mastery: Needs, Value, ROI, and Business Cases

Purpose
Design and run a sales system that reliably converts attention into revenue. This doctrine covers:
prospecting, outreach, appointment setting, qualification, discovery, pitching, objection handling,
closing, referrals, and scaling through process + tools + management.

Core Sales System (End-to-End)
1) Targeting: define ICP + buying triggers + reachable channels.
2) Prospecting: build and prioritize lead lists with evidence-based intent signals.
3) Outreach: run a multi-channel cadence to earn a conversation.
4) Qualification: confirm fit, urgency, authority, and economic impact.
5) Discovery: quantify pain and map decision process.
6) Proposal/Demo: present a tailored solution with proof and clear next steps.
7) Negotiation/Close: reduce risk, remove friction, finalize terms, get signature.
8) Post-Sale: drive success, expansion, referrals, and durable relationships.

Operating Rules
1) Activity is a leading indicator; conversion is the constraint.
2) Sales is structured learning: every step produces data that improves the next step.
3) Control is earned via clarity: agenda, next steps, and mutual commitments.
4) Qualification is disqualification. Saying “no” early increases win rate.
5) Pipeline is a model. Forecasting improves when stage definitions are strict.

Scope
Discovery is the engine of persuasion. It converts curiosity into urgency and aligns stakeholders.

1) Discovery Principles
• Ask to understand, not to trap.
• Quantify impact. Numbers create urgency.
• Map the decision. Deals are systems.
• Synthesize often: “Here’s what I’m hearing…”

2) SPIN Questioning (Consultative Discovery)
Situation: context without drowning in details
Problem: what is broken or constrained
Implication: why it matters, second-order costs
Need-payoff: what improvement is worth and why now

3) Sandler “Pain Funnel” Logic (Root Cause)
Move from surface pain to deep pain:
• “Tell me more”
• “How long has that been happening”
• “What have you tried”
• “What happens if nothing changes”
Goal: uncover cost of inaction and emotional stakes.

4) Business Case Construction
Impact categories
• revenue gain
• cost reduction
• time saved
• risk reduced
• compliance improved
Business case template
1) baseline
2) target
3) delta
4) mechanism (how solution creates delta)
5) implementation cost
6) payback period

5) Stakeholder Map
Roles
• Economic buyer: owns budget
• Champion: internal seller
• User: daily operator
• Influencer: shapes criteria
• Gatekeeper/procurement: controls process
Output: stakeholder influence map + messaging per role.

6) Decision Process Mapping
Ask:
• “Walk me through how decisions like this get made.”
• “What are the steps and who signs off.”
• “What could slow this down.”
Capture:
• steps
• meeting schedule
• evaluation criteria
• procurement and legal path (paper process)

Deliverables
1) discovery call agenda + script
2) SPIN question bank by segment
3) pain funnel prompts
4) business case worksheet
5) stakeholder and decision maps
