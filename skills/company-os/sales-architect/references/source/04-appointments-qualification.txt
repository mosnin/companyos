SALES ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 04: Appointments, Qualification, and Deal Scoping

Purpose
Design and run a sales system that reliably converts attention into revenue. This doctrine covers:
prospecting, outreach, appointment setting, qualification, discovery, pitching, objection handling,
closing, referrals, and scaling through process + tools + management.

Core Sales System (End-to-End)
1) Targeting: define ICP + buying triggers + reachable channels.
2) Prospecting: build and prioritize lead lists with evidence-based intent signals.
3) Outreach: run a multi-channel cadence to earn a conversation.
4) Qualification: confirm fit, urgency, authority, and economic impact.
5) Discovery: quantify pain and map decision process.
6) Proposal/Demo: present a tailored solution with proof and clear next steps.
7) Negotiation/Close: reduce risk, remove friction, finalize terms, get signature.
8) Post-Sale: drive success, expansion, referrals, and durable relationships.

Operating Rules
1) Activity is a leading indicator; conversion is the constraint.
2) Sales is structured learning: every step produces data that improves the next step.
3) Control is earned via clarity: agenda, next steps, and mutual commitments.
4) Qualification is disqualification. Saying “no” early increases win rate.
5) Pipeline is a model. Forecasting improves when stage definitions are strict.

Scope
Turn conversations into scheduled meetings, then qualify and scope opportunities with consistency.

1) Appointment Setting (Meeting Conversion)
Goal: secure a specific next step with time and agenda.
Appointment close options
• “Does Tue 10am or Wed 2pm work for a 20-min call?”
• “If I show you X, and it’s relevant, are you open to Y next?”
Reduce friction:
• offer shorter first call
• offer “audit” or “assessment”
• send calendar invite immediately
• confirm 24 hours prior

2) Qualification: Fast and Deep
Use a framework matched to complexity:
Light qualification
• BANT: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline
Consultative qualification
• GPCTBA/C&I: Goals, Plans, Challenges, Timeline, Budget, Authority, Consequences & Implications
Complex B2B qualification
• MEDDIC/MEDDPICC: Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Paper Process, Implication of Pain, Champion, Competition
Alternative modern qualification
• NEAT: Need, Economic Impact, Access to Authority, Timeline

3) Qualification Outputs (What “Good” Looks Like)
A qualified opportunity has:
• ICP fit confirmed
• quantified impact (money/time/risk)
• a defined success outcome
• stakeholders mapped (economic buyer + champion)
• decision criteria and process documented
• timeline hypothesis and next step locked

4) Deal Scoping
Scope = what you will do, what is excluded, who owns what, and timeline.
Scoping method:
1) current state map
2) desired state
3) gap analysis
4) requirements (must/should/could)
5) dependencies and risks
6) success criteria and measurement

5) Disqualification (Professional No)
Disqualify when:
• no clear problem
• no timeline and no trigger
• no access to authority path
• no economic justification
• misaligned expectations
Deliverable: “close the loop” message with a clean door-open for later.

Deliverables
1) appointment setting scripts
2) qualification checklists by framework
3) scope template (current → future → gap → plan)
4) disqualification scripts and recycle rules
