SALES ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 06: Pitches, Demos, and Presentations: Story, Proof, and Control

Purpose
Design and run a sales system that reliably converts attention into revenue. This doctrine covers:
prospecting, outreach, appointment setting, qualification, discovery, pitching, objection handling,
closing, referrals, and scaling through process + tools + management.

Core Sales System (End-to-End)
1) Targeting: define ICP + buying triggers + reachable channels.
2) Prospecting: build and prioritize lead lists with evidence-based intent signals.
3) Outreach: run a multi-channel cadence to earn a conversation.
4) Qualification: confirm fit, urgency, authority, and economic impact.
5) Discovery: quantify pain and map decision process.
6) Proposal/Demo: present a tailored solution with proof and clear next steps.
7) Negotiation/Close: reduce risk, remove friction, finalize terms, get signature.
8) Post-Sale: drive success, expansion, referrals, and durable relationships.

Operating Rules
1) Activity is a leading indicator; conversion is the constraint.
2) Sales is structured learning: every step produces data that improves the next step.
3) Control is earned via clarity: agenda, next steps, and mutual commitments.
4) Qualification is disqualification. Saying “no” early increases win rate.
5) Pipeline is a model. Forecasting improves when stage definitions are strict.

============================================================
Scope
How to present without “feature dumping”: narrative, proof, and controlled next steps.

1) Pitch Architecture (High-Level)
A strong pitch answers:
1) what problem is this
2) why it matters now
3) what changes with you
4) why you are credible
5) what happens next

2) Challenger Pattern (Teach, Tailor, Take Control)
Teach: reframe their problem with an insight
Tailor: connect to their context and metrics
Take control: guide to a decision and next steps

3) Demo Framework (Show Value, Not UI)
Demo = claim → proof → confirmation.
Structure
1) recap goals and success criteria
2) show the “before” problem
3) show the “after” workflow
4) prove key claims (data, case studies)
5) confirm fit and next step

4) Presentation Tools
• “message map”: 1 core claim, 3 pillars, proof per pillar
• “objection slide”: name risk, show mitigation
• “implementation slide”: timeline, owners, dependencies
• “ROI slide”: baseline, delta, payback

5) Control: Agenda and Mutual Commitments
Start every meeting with:
• time check
• purpose
• agenda
• outcomes and next step options

Deliverables
1) pitch template and talk track
2) challenger reframe templates
3) demo checklist + script
4) slide outline: message map, ROI, implementation, risk
5) meeting control opener and closer
