## Allocation and drift controls

- Default allocation: 65% transformative capability delivery, 20% frontier bets/prototypes, 10% direct enablers, 5% audit/maintenance.
- Require an explicit P0 customer-safety exception to exceed the enabler or maintenance allocation.
- A frontier bet may begin with an unknown feature name or implementation. It needs a point of view, a prototype, a learning signal, a cap, and a scale/pivot/kill decision—not a pre-existing requirement.
- Create a drift event after two accepted tasks without visible milestone movement.
- Do not permit a no-op while a committed milestone or bounded frontier bet has a safe next action.

## Review record

For every review record: product-reality evidence reviewed, opportunity or requirement advanced, technology decision, capability delivered, demo/evidence, experience score, cost, rejected alternatives, risks, and exact next action.
