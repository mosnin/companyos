MARKETING OS — SECTION 04: Context and Memory

Purpose
Compound brand and campaign learning without re-explaining the company
every session and without pasting memory files into prompts.

Locators, not essays
- Brand system: asset locator from `$brand-creative-system` /
  `$company-blueprint` brand references.
- Voice: compact always/never tokens plus two approved examples.
  Bind a digest. Do not paste the voice file.
- Audience profile: digest of the current profile artifact.
- Campaign history: knowledge source id. Load only the rows that
  match this segment or channel.
- Working queue: the manager charter and work graph. Do not keep a
  second markdown task OS.

Write-back
After review, record what ran, what evidence said, and the continue /
change / stop decision as a knowledge or asset record with a digest.
Corrections become durable rules only after they are written as
accepted knowledge, not as chat residue.

Isolation
Each Company OS project instance keeps its own marketing context
inside `.company-os/`. Do not mix client ledgers. Do not store
credentials in brand or voice files.

Token rule
The worker packet may include the schema id, the required locators,
and the upstream digest. It may not include this source pack,
`$company-os`, `$elastic-company-os`, or a brand essay.

End of file.
