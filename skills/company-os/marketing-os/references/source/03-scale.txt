MARKETING OS — SECTION 03: Scale

Purpose
Grow marketing output without growing an agent org chart. Scale by
work-graph width: independently accountable outcomes and worker packets.

What does not scale
- One orchestrator plus three named specialists as always-on agents.
- A new agent per channel, client, or content format.
- Pasting brand, voice, history, and wisdom into every prompt.
- Implicit skill invocation.
- Cloning the whole Marketing OS per client.

What does scale
- One middle marketing manager per department outcome.
- One low-level campaign manager only when a campaign or client has
  its own acceptance, budget, and kill rule. Peer Sol managers under
  master. Not a parent hop.
- Staff templates: research, brief, copy. Clone a slot per packet.
  Slot count is not a dispatch quota. The accepted work graph decides
  how many packets run.
- Parallel campaigns isolate context by digest. Campaign A must not
  receive Campaign B's brief.
- Multi-brand: one `client_locator` and `brand_locator` per packet.
  Do not stand up a second operating system.
- New channels are playbook variants or later skills, not new fabric
  roles. Paid, SEO, and CRO wait until the base pipeline produces
  accepted artifacts.
- Live analytics and CRM stay integrations with
  `requires-host-preflight`. Unchecked tools are unavailable.

Admission
Do not open a second campaign manager until the first campaign has a
brief digest and a named bottleneck. Tokens follow the global
bottleneck, not the number of channels.
