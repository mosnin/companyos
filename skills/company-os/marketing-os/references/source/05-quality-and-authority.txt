MARKETING OS — SECTION 05: Quality and Authority

Purpose
Keep marketing useful, honest, and inside Company OS authority.

Quality
- Revenue-linked object: qualified demand, conversion, CAC, payback.
  Impressions and "awareness" are sensors, not success.
- Decision-stage evidence first. A comparison or objection page that
  a buyer uses to choose beats a high-traffic definition article.
- Quoted language must be cited. If the quote cannot be sourced, it
  is not voice-of-customer.
- Hooks are a testable claim, a specific number, and a next action.
  A slogan is not a hook.
- Activation follows observed behavior: no action, one action, repeat
  action. Each state gets a different next packet.
- Control scores current artifact bytes with
  `$execute-outcome-evaluator`. Taste is not a score.

Authority
- Recommend only. Approval is required for customer communication,
  public publishing, pricing claims, and spend.
- Do not mutate CRM, send email, post, or buy media from this
  overlay.
- Do not fabricate client names, revenue, or case results.
- Sales accepts the qualified-lead definition. Marketing may not
  redefine a lead to make volume look qualified.
- Brand Creative accepts brand-system changes. Marketing may not
  silently restyle the company.
- Any manager escalates to master. A peer notice is not command.

Stop
If proof is missing, the channel is unapproved, the brief digest
drifted, or the request needs publish/spend, stop. Rejected commands
must not mutate governed state.

End of file.
