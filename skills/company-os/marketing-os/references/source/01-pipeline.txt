MARKETING OS — SECTION 01: Pipeline

Purpose
Replace an always-on marketing orchestrator with a four-stage playbook
that any Sol manager can run. The manager names the stage. Workers
produce one artifact. Schemas carry context between packets.

Stages
1. Research. Produce `audience-profile` or `research-report`. Every
   finding is sourced and confidence-rated. Decision-stage evidence
   outranks top-of-funnel volume.
2. Brief. Consume the research digest. Produce `campaign-brief` with a
   measurable goal, one promise, proof, deliverables, kill rule, and
   Sales-accepted qualified-lead definition.
3. Copy. Consume the brief digest. Produce `copy-packet` for one
   format. No brief digest, no copy.
4. Review. Score the artifact bytes. Continue, change, or stop. Record
   the result as campaign-history knowledge, not a chat.

Routing
The incoming request names a stage or a full campaign. A full campaign
is the four stages in order. A research-only request stops after stage
1. A copy request with no brief digest returns to stage 2.

Ambiguity
Ask one question: research, brief, copy, or review? Do not guess.
Do not spawn a router agent to ask it.

What the starter got right
Sequence plus structured handoff beats one-shot prose. Keep that.
Drop the specialist who only routes. Routing is the playbook.

Company OS rule
Executable delegation stays master → manager → worker. The Marketing
manager is not an orchestrator persona and not a fourth hop.
`$force-first-execution` still wins: the first artifact is the
research packet or the brief, not a workflow memo.
