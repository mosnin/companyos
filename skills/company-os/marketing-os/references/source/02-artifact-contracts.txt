MARKETING OS — SECTION 02: Artifact Contracts

Purpose
Handoffs are content-addressed JSON contracts, not pasted chat. A
downstream packet names the upstream digest. If the digest is missing
or drifted, the packet is invalid.

Contracts
- `company-os.marketing-audience-profile.v1`
  Who, job, pains, quoted language with sources, alternatives,
  confidence, gaps. Compact. Decision-useful fields only.
- `company-os.marketing-research-report.v1`
  One research question. At most five ranked findings. Each finding
  has source, confidence, and a strategic implication.
- `company-os.marketing-campaign-brief.v1`
  Goal that is not awareness. Audience digest. Promise.
  Differentiator. Proof. Deliverables. Constraints. Kill rule.
  Qualified-lead definition. Brand and voice locators.
- `company-os.marketing-copy-packet.v1`
  Brief digest. One format. Body. Claims with sources. CTA. Voice
  check against the voice locator.

Validate with
`python3 scripts/validate_marketing_os.py --root <skill-root>`

Rules
- Do not hand a worker the previous packet's prose. Hand the digest
  and the exact schema id.
- Do not invent extra required fields to look complete. Completeness
  is for the decision.
- A brief without a kill rule is not a brief.
- A copy packet whose brief digest does not match current brief bytes
  is rejected.
- Examples live in `assets/examples/`. They are fixtures, not live
  campaigns.

End of file.
