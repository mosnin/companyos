MARKETING OS — SOURCE PACK INDEX

This kit is a compiled Company OS thinking overlay for the Marketing
department. It compiles the useful architecture of the public
marketing-os-starter repository: a research → brief → copy pipeline,
structured handoffs, and compounding brand context.

It is not a copy of that repository's prompts, agent souls, hook
formulas, or growth-playbook text. It is not an affiliation with its
authors or agency. Load one primary file for the named decision. Do
not invent a fourth executable hop.

Decision -> file
- Pipeline and stage gates -> 01-pipeline.txt
- Artifact contracts and digests -> 02-artifact-contracts.txt
- How marketing scales -> 03-scale.txt
- Context locators and memory -> 04-context-and-memory.txt
- Quality, sales handoff, authority -> 05-quality-and-authority.txt

Shared operating rules
- The playbook routes. Do not spawn an orchestrator agent.
- Research packet → brief contract → copy packet → review receipt.
- Staff slots are research, brief, and copy. They are Luna workers.
- Scale by work-graph width, not by named specialist agents.
- Pass locators and digests. Do not paste this pack.
- Awareness is not a KPI. Qualified demand is.
- Do not fabricate proofs. Do not publish, send, or spend from this
  overlay.
- `$manage-company-program` still wins for delivery.
- `$force-first-execution` still wins for motion.
