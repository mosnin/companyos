# Marketing OS

This is a thinking overlay for the Marketing department. It does not own authority, leases, fabric, spend, or completion. `$manage-company-program`, `$company-blueprint`, `$department-charters`, `$corporate-departments`, `$corporate-management`, `$brand-creative-system`, `$govern-outcome-execution`, and `$force-first-execution` still win.

This doctrine is an original compiled operating template. It compiles the useful architecture of a public marketing-os-starter (research → brief → copy, schema handoffs, compounding context). It is not a copy of that repository's prompts or frameworks, and not an affiliation with its authors.

Do not send this skill to Luna workers. Do not use it as the master persona. Do not attach it to a manager whose outcome is not marketing.

## Every heartbeat

Before directing marketing work, answer these:

1. Name the stage: research, brief, copy, or review. Do not start copy without a brief digest.
2. Name the economic object: qualified demand Sales will accept, at a stated acquisition cost and payback. Awareness is not the object.
3. Name the context locators: brand, voice, audience profile, campaign history. Pass digests, not the operating system.
4. Name the next staff slot. Research, brief, and copy are worker templates, not extra managers.
5. Pause if this is a fourth hop, an orchestrator agent, a worker setting department strategy, a fabricated proof, or an unapproved publish, send, spend, or CRM write.

Do not publish, send, spend, mutate CRM, or enable the scheduler or runtime from this overlay.

## Forced moves

- Pipeline is research packet → brief contract → copy packet → review receipt. The playbook is the router. Do not spawn an orchestrator.
- Scale by independently accountable campaigns and worker packets. Do not add a specialist agent per channel or client.
- One middle marketing manager owns the department outcome. A low-level campaign manager exists only when one campaign or client is independently accountable. Those managers are peers under master.
- Do not write copy without a brief digest. Do not brief without an audience-profile digest or an explicit research gap.
- Every claim needs a source. Missing evidence is a gap, never a number.
- Define a Sales-accepted qualified lead before paid spend. Marketing does not own the close.
- Brand Creative owns the brand system. Marketing uses locators.
- Tokens follow the global bottleneck. Do not paste this pack, `$company-os`, or `$elastic-company-os`.
- Control scores artifact bytes through `$execute-outcome-evaluator`. A manager taste score is not a score.
- `$force-first-execution` still wins. A framework memo without a candidate is not progress.

## Artifacts

Keep these manager-local and compact:

- stage and next slot
- audience-profile, research-report, campaign-brief, or copy-packet digest
- economic object, kill rule, and required approval
