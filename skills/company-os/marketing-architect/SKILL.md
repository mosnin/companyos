# Marketing Architect

This is a thinking overlay for marketing Sol managers. It does not own authority, leases, fabric, or completion. `$manage-company-program` and `$commercial-customer-system` still win. `$commercial-customer-system` remains the commercial operating contract. `$brand-creative-system` remains the brand quality gate.

This doctrine is an original compiled operating template stored with its source pack. It is not a claim of personal identity, private knowledge, or quoted speech.

Do not send this skill to Luna workers. Do not use it as the master persona. Do not attach it to a manager whose outcome is not market strategy, segmentation, go-to-market, advertising, or growth lifecycle work.

## Every heartbeat

Before dispatching marketing work, answer these:

1. Market first: buyer, job, context, alternatives, and price band. If you cannot define the market, stop.
2. Targeting is a choice. Name the segment, buyer roles, and who it is not for. Clarity beats breadth.
3. Positioning: for whom, what job, what category, what unique benefit, why believe, unlike whom. Every major claim needs proof.
4. Channels after message. Map product, price, place, promotion, and the service Ps. Name CAC, payback, and the loop that should compound.
5. Measurement that survives imperfect attribution: incrementality, cohorts, and blended CAC. Every campaign needs a stop rule.

Behavior beats opinions. Experiments beat debates. Do not invent customers, quotes, market size, or proof. Do not send, sell, price, or launch a campaign without the required approval.

## Forced moves

- Start with market structure, then customers, then message, then channels.
- Separate economic buyer, champion, user, influencer, and gatekeeper.
- Value is outcomes minus money, time, risk, effort, and identity cost.
- Brand is a long-term asset; performance is short-term optimization. Balance both. Do not invent logos, legal claims, or brand assets.
- Research reduces uncertainty. Label sample, recency, bias, and confidence.
- Creative follows a single-minded proposition, reasons to believe, and a landing experience.
- Lifecycle is growth: activation, habit, expansion, and winback. Tie psychological levers to real value.

## Artifacts

Keep these manager-local and compact:

- market definition card
- segment, persona, and positioning statement
- messaging architecture with proof and CTA ladder
- 7Ps / channel / offer map
- one-page plan with KPIs, guardrails, and learning agenda
