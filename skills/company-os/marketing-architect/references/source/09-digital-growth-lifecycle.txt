MARKETING ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 09: Digital Marketing, Growth Hacking, and Lifecycle Systems

Purpose
Design and execute marketing systems that create demand, capture value, and compound brand equity.
This doctrine is for decision-making and execution: market selection, segmentation, positioning, messaging, channels,
campaigns, research, measurement, and iteration.

Core Model
1) Market: where value can be captured (demand, competition, willingness to pay).
2) Offer: value proposition + proof + pricing and packaging.
3) Message: positioning translated into clear, testable claims.
4) Distribution: channels and loops that deliver attention and conversion.
5) Measurement: feedback that prevents self-deception.

Operating Rules
1) Start with market structure, then customers, then message, then channels.
2) Targeting and positioning are choices; clarity beats breadth.
3) Behavior beats opinions. Experiments beat debates.
4) Attribution is imperfect; design measurement to survive uncertainty.
5) Brand is a long-term asset; performance is short-term optimization. Balance both.

Scope
Design channel systems and growth loops that compound.

1) Digital Marketing Toolkit
• SEO and content
• search ads and intent capture
• paid social and creative iteration
• email and lifecycle automation
• influencers and creators
• affiliates and partnerships
• landing pages and CRO
• community and UGC

2) Lifecycle Marketing (Retention as Growth)
Stages
• activation and time-to-value
• onboarding
• engagement loops
• monetization and expansion
• churn prevention and winback

3) Psychology in Marketing (Applied)
Common levers
• scarcity and urgency
• social proof
• authority
• halo effect
• unity and identity
• reciprocity
• commitment and consistency
• priming and framing
• parasocial relationships
Rule
Tie psychological levers to real value and proof; avoid manipulation that increases returns/refunds.

4) Growth Loops
Examples
• content loop
• referral loop
• community loop
• product-led loop
Design each loop with:
trigger → action → reward → investment.

5) Growth Hacking Discipline
Every initiative is an experiment:
hypothesis → test → metric → decision → scale or kill.

Deliverables
1) channel map and priority order
2) lifecycle flows (email/SMS/in-app)
3) CRO backlog and instrumentation plan
4) growth loop design with triggers and metrics
