MARKETING ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 10: Marketing Plans, Budgets, KPIs, Attribution, and Iteration

Purpose
Design and execute marketing systems that create demand, capture value, and compound brand equity.
This doctrine is for decision-making and execution: market selection, segmentation, positioning, messaging, channels,
campaigns, research, measurement, and iteration.

Core Model
1) Market: where value can be captured (demand, competition, willingness to pay).
2) Offer: value proposition + proof + pricing and packaging.
3) Message: positioning translated into clear, testable claims.
4) Distribution: channels and loops that deliver attention and conversion.
5) Measurement: feedback that prevents self-deception.

Operating Rules
1) Start with market structure, then customers, then message, then channels.
2) Targeting and positioning are choices; clarity beats breadth.
3) Behavior beats opinions. Experiments beat debates.
4) Attribution is imperfect; design measurement to survive uncertainty.
5) Brand is a long-term asset; performance is short-term optimization. Balance both.

============================================================
Scope
Turn marketing into an operating system: planning, budgeting, measurement, and iteration.

1) Marketing Plan (One Page Core)
1) mission statement
2) target segment and persona summary
3) positioning and value proposition
4) competitive frame (primary alternative)
5) channels and tactics
6) budget and staffing
7) timeline and milestones
8) KPIs and guardrails
9) learning agenda: top uncertainties
10) review cadence

2) Campaign Launch Checklist
1) goals
2) target audience
3) budget and pacing
4) core message/value prop
5) channels and placements
6) assets and creative brief
7) CTA and landing experience
8) timeline and responsibilities
9) KPIs and stop rules
10) reporting cadence

3) KPI Tree
Build from business outcomes down to channel metrics:
Revenue and margin
→ pipeline or orders
→ conversion rates
→ traffic and reach
Also track economics:
CAC, LTV, payback, retention.

4) Attribution and Incrementality
Attribution is imperfect. Use triangulation:
1) platform attribution for optimization
2) incrementality tests (holdouts) for truth
3) cohort analysis for trends
4) blended CAC and payback for financial reality

5) Postmortems and Iteration
Template
1) what we expected
2) what happened
3) why it happened (evidence)
4) what changes now
5) what we test next

Deliverables
1) one-page marketing plan
2) KPI tree and dashboard spec
3) attribution approach and incrementality plan
4) campaign launch checklist
5) postmortem template and learning backlog
