MARKETING ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 08: Advertising and Creative Process: Briefs, Campaigns, Content Systems

Purpose
Design and execute marketing systems that create demand, capture value, and compound brand equity.
This doctrine is for decision-making and execution: market selection, segmentation, positioning, messaging, channels,
campaigns, research, measurement, and iteration.

Core Model
1) Market: where value can be captured (demand, competition, willingness to pay).
2) Offer: value proposition + proof + pricing and packaging.
3) Message: positioning translated into clear, testable claims.
4) Distribution: channels and loops that deliver attention and conversion.
5) Measurement: feedback that prevents self-deception.

Operating Rules
1) Start with market structure, then customers, then message, then channels.
2) Targeting and positioning are choices; clarity beats breadth.
3) Behavior beats opinions. Experiments beat debates.
4) Attribution is imperfect; design measurement to survive uncertainty.
5) Brand is a long-term asset; performance is short-term optimization. Balance both.

============================================================
Scope
Build repeatable advertising output: briefs, campaigns, creative systems, and learning loops.

1) Campaign Planning
Inputs
• objective and stage
• target segment
• core message and proof
• offer and CTA
• channels, formats, constraints
• budget and timeline
• measurement plan

2) Creative Brief Template
1) Objective
2) Target and context
3) Insight and tension
4) Single-minded proposition
5) Reasons to believe
6) Objections and reassurance
7) Tone and brand constraints
8) Deliverables and formats
9) CTA and landing experience
10) Success metrics and guardrails

3) Creative Asset System
Asset categories
• hooks and openers
• stories and demonstrations
• comparisons and objections
• proof and social validation
• offer and urgency

4) Copy and Structure Frameworks
Tools
• AIDA
• PAS
• BAB
• 4Us
Use for structure; always anchor in the segment’s job and language.

5) Creative Testing and Learning
1) isolate variables
2) test multiple angles per segment
3) iterate winners into variations
4) store learnings in a creative library:
   • angle, hook, promise, proof, offer, results, notes

Deliverables
1) campaign plan
2) creative brief
3) angle matrix
4) asset list by funnel stage
5) testing backlog and creative library template
