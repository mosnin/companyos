# Brand Architect

This is a thinking overlay for brand-system Sol managers. It does not own authority, leases, fabric, or completion. `$manage-company-program` and `$brand-creative-system` still win. `$brand-creative-system` remains the brand operating contract and quality gate.

This doctrine is an original compiled operating template stored with its source pack.

Do not send this skill to Luna workers. Do not use it as the master persona. Do not attach it to a manager whose outcome is not brand system, positioning, identity, voice, presence, or brand governance.

## Every heartbeat

Before dispatching brand work, answer these:

1. Can you state the brand as meaning, promise, proof, and signals. If any of the four is missing, stop.
2. Positioning: for whom, what job, what category, what benefit, why believe, unlike whom.
3. Audience: ICP, job to be done, language they use, and the emotional outcome with proof.
4. Pillars: three to five stable promises. Each has meaning, who it is for, what it is not, and proof.
5. Attract, engage, delight. Name the next touchpoint that must stay coherent.

Do not invent customer claims, logos, legal claims, or brand assets. Every major claim needs proof. Distinguish evidence from creative conviction.

## Forced moves

- Identity, credibility, and visibility are the core. Authenticity and consistency are constraints, not style extras.
- Build the six-part strategy skeleton: positioning, equity pyramid, pillars, expansion zones, guidelines, architecture.
- Voice is stable. Tone is situational. Keep an approved message library; banned phrases are part of the design.
- Visuals are distinctive assets plus repetition, not decoration. Experience principles apply across product, support, and presence.
- Presence follows the same promise. Distribution that cannot carry the proof is noise.
- Govern the system: branded house vs house of brands, naming rules, approvals, and equity layers from awareness to advocacy.
- Expand only in core, adjacent, or stretch zones with an explicit dilution test.

## Artifacts

Keep these manager-local and compact:

- one-page brand system map
- positioning statement and 2x2
- pillar and proof library
- voice and message architecture
- governance and architecture decision
