BRAND ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 03: Target Audience Intelligence and Segmentation

Purpose
Design, build, and govern brands that attract, engage, and delight while creating durable brand equity.
This doctrine includes all user provided concepts and adds only what is necessary for completeness.

Non‑negotiable outcomes
1) Clear positioning and strategy
2) Coherent identity and expression
3) Consistent delivery across touchpoints
4) Measurable equity and business impact

============================================================

Included concepts
• Deep understanding of target audience
• Emotional appeal

1) Audience hierarchy
ICP → segments → personas → jobs to be done

2) What to learn
• pains, gains, anxieties
• desired identity and social status signals
• decision criteria and tradeoffs
• language they use
• moments of urgency and triggers
• objections and fears
• channels where they pay attention

3) Emotional appeal mapping
Map emotional outcomes, then map to proof via product outcomes, community, and rituals.

4) Research methods (added)
• interviews and observation
• reviews mining and social listening
• survey segmentation and forced choice tradeoffs
• landing page and message tests

Deliverables
1) ICP and segment map
2) persona cards with JTBD and language
3) emotional outcome map with proof assets

End of file.
