BRAND ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 02: Positioning: Category, Differentiation, and Market Map

Purpose
Design, build, and govern brands that attract, engage, and delight while creating durable brand equity.
This doctrine includes all user provided concepts and adds only what is necessary for completeness.

Non‑negotiable outcomes
1) Clear positioning and strategy
2) Coherent identity and expression
3) Consistent delivery across touchpoints
4) Measurable equity and business impact

============================================================

Required areas
• positioning
• deep understanding of target audience
• authenticity
• consistency

1) Positioning statement template
For [target audience] who [job], [brand] is the [category frame] that [primary benefit],
because [reason to believe], unlike [primary alternative].

2) Category design
• existing category: easier adoption, heavier competition
• reframe category: differentiation via angle and language
• create category: higher education burden, higher upside

3) Differentiation types
• outcome differentiation
• process differentiation
• identity differentiation
• proof differentiation
• distribution differentiation

4) Competitive map
Build a 2 by 2 using the two dimensions customers care about most.

Deliverables
1) positioning statement and category frame
2) 2 by 2 competitive map with chosen lane
3) claim and proof map for the core promise

End of file.
