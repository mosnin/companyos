BRAND ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 06: Visual Identity System: Logo, Color, Typography, and Assets

Purpose
Design, build, and govern brands that attract, engage, and delight while creating durable brand equity.
This doctrine includes all user provided concepts and adds only what is necessary for completeness.

Non‑negotiable outcomes
1) Clear positioning and strategy
2) Coherent identity and expression
3) Consistent delivery across touchpoints
4) Measurable equity and business impact

============================================================

Included concepts
• Principles of logo (simple, memorable, timeless, versatile, appropriate)
• Consistent color palette
• Brand visuals
• Design parameters

1) Logo principles (kept)
simple, memorable, timeless, versatile, appropriate

2) Color system
Primary, secondary, neutrals, documented values and usage rules.

3) Typography (added)
Primary typeface, secondary typeface, hierarchy, spacing rules.

4) Asset library
Icons, photography, illustration, patterns, layout grids, motion rules.

5) Design parameters
Spacing scale, radius and stroke rules, shadow rules, imagery rules.

Deliverables
1) visual identity spec sheet
2) palette with roles and rules
3) typography hierarchy spec
4) asset library checklist

End of file.
