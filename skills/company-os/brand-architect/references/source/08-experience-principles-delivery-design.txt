BRAND ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 08: Experience Principles and Delivery Design

Purpose
Design, build, and govern brands that attract, engage, and delight while creating durable brand equity.
This doctrine includes all user provided concepts and adds only what is necessary for completeness.

Non‑negotiable outcomes
1) Clear positioning and strategy
2) Coherent identity and expression
3) Consistent delivery across touchpoints
4) Measurable equity and business impact

============================================================

Included concepts
• Experience principles
• A brand to attract, engage, and delight
• Consistency

1) Experience principles
3 to 7 rules for how the brand feels in use. Define meaning, manifestations, violations.

2) Journey blueprint (added)
Awareness, consideration, purchase, onboarding, usage, support, renewal.
Define goal, friction, emotion, brand moment, success metric per stage.

3) Delight design
Relevant, proportional, consistent with identity.

Deliverables
1) experience principles doc
2) journey blueprint with brand moments
3) delight playbook mapped to stages
