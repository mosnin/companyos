BRAND ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 10: Governance, Architecture, Equity, and Category Expansion

Purpose
Design, build, and govern brands that attract, engage, and delight while creating durable brand equity.
This doctrine includes all user provided concepts and adds only what is necessary for completeness.

Non‑negotiable outcomes
1) Clear positioning and strategy
2) Coherent identity and expression
3) Consistent delivery across touchpoints
4) Measurable equity and business impact

============================================================

Included concepts
• equity pyramid
• rule of the road or guidelines
• architecture
• category expansion zones

1) Brand equity pyramid (operational)
Awareness, meaning, performance perceptions, preference, loyalty, advocacy.
Track metrics per layer.

2) Rule of the road or guidelines
What is allowed, forbidden, approvals, asset creation process.

3) Brand architecture
Branded house, house of brands, endorsed brands.
Define naming rules and alignment levels.

4) Category expansion zones
Core, adjacent, stretch. Define fit, capability upgrades, dilution risk, proof required.

Deliverables
1) governance rules and approval flow
2) architecture decision and naming rules
3) expansion map and zone tests
4) equity dashboard and cadence

End of file.
