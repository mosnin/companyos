BRAND ARCHITECT DOCTRINE — SECTION 07: Brand Voice and Messaging System

Purpose
Design, build, and govern brands that attract, engage, and delight while creating durable brand equity.
This doctrine includes all user provided concepts and adds only what is necessary for completeness.

Non‑negotiable outcomes
1) Clear positioning and strategy
2) Coherent identity and expression
3) Consistent delivery across touchpoints
4) Measurable equity and business impact

============================================================

Included concepts
• Brand voice
• Consistency

1) Voice vs tone
Voice is stable personality. Tone is situational modulation.

2) Voice pillars
3 to 5 traits with do and do not examples, preferred vocabulary, banned phrases, rhythm rules.

3) Message architecture (added)
One sentence value prop, tagline, elevator pitch, proof points, objection responses, microcopy rules.

4) Messaging library
Approved claims, approved proof, approved phrases, version controlled.

Deliverables
1) voice guide
2) message architecture set
3) messaging library template
