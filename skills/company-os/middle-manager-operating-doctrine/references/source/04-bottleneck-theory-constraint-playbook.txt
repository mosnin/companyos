============================================================

Core rule
Production rate equals the weakest process step.

Bottleneck identification
• Where work piles up.
• Where cycle time is longest.
• Where defects or rework concentrate.
• Where dependency waits dominate.

Constraint playbook
1) Measure throughput and cycle time per step.
2) Identify the single constraint.
3) Exploit the constraint: keep it always working on the highest-value tasks.
4) Subordinate everything else to the constraint.
5) Elevate the constraint: add capacity, change architecture, remove step.
6) Repeat. The constraint moves.

Resource allocation rule
Resources follow the constraint, not hierarchy.
