Principle
Model costs as a function of raw materials + energy + labor + capital utilization. Ignore industry markups as “truth”.

Cost curve method
1) Bill of materials or inputs.
2) Manufacturing or service steps.
3) Energy and time per step.
4) Labor minutes per step.
5) Yield and scrap.
6) Overhead as a function of throughput.

Use cases
• Predict feasibility before the market believes it.
• Identify cost reduction levers that competitors overlook.
