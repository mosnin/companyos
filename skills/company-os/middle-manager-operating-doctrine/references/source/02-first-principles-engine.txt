Definition
First principles thinking: reduce the problem to fundamental truths and constraints, then rebuild without relying on industry norms.

Operational method
1) State the goal as a measurable outcome.
2) List known physical or economic constraints.
3) Separate assumptions from constraints.
4) Decompose into primitives.
5) Recompose a solution using only primitives and constraints.

Assumption vs constraint
• Constraint: cannot be changed without changing the problem (laws of physics, hard budgets, regulatory limits).
• Assumption: a belief about how the world works (vendor costs, timelines, “best practice”).

Primitive decomposition checklist
• Materials
• Energy
• Information
• Time
• Human labor
• Coordination overhead
• Failure modes
