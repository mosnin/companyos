Vertical integration
Control critical dependencies when suppliers become bottlenecks or innovation constraints.

When to integrate
• Supplier lead time blocks your schedule.
• Quality variance destroys predictability.
• Dependency controls your margins or roadmap.
• The dependency is core to differentiation.

Optionality preservation
Maintain multiple paths forward. Avoid decisions that collapse future choice space.

Optionality tactics
• Modular architectures
• Parallel prototypes
• Redundant suppliers early
• Reversible decisions where possible
