Expected value
EV = probability of success × magnitude of outcome − probability of failure × magnitude of loss

Rules
• Prefer bets with asymmetric upside and capped downside.
• Avoid symmetrical risk profiles unless required.
• Update probabilities based on new evidence, not hope.

Risk asymmetry lens
• Downside: cash burn, time, reputation, lock-in.
• Upside: compounding advantage, optionality expansion, moat creation.

Decision protocol
1) Enumerate options.
2) Assign base-rate priors.
3) Identify key uncertainties.
4) Design tests that most change the posterior.
5) Choose the option with best EV adjusted for survivability.
