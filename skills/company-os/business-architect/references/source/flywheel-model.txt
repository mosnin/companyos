  Identify and design self-reinforcing growth loops

    reinforce inputs, increasing momentum with each cycle.

    description: Key activities or outcomes in the loop.
  links:
    description: How one node strengthens the next.

    - "Friction must be reduced continuously"
    - "Momentum compounds non-linearly"

  - flywheel_diagram_nodes
  - reinforcing_loops
  - friction_points
  - compounding_metrics
