# Brand and Creative System

Create a single, living expression system rather than disconnected copy or visual polish.

## Operating contract

Capture the audience, category tension, point of view, promise, proof, personality, visual/motion principles, language rules, and prohibited patterns. Distinguish evidence from creative conviction. Do not invent customer claims, logos, legal claims, or brand assets.

## Workflow

1. **Extract:** inspect approved project artifacts, product behavior, customer language, and market context; label unknowns.
2. **Frame:** create a concise brand thesis: who the product is for, the before/after, why it is uniquely credible, and the emotional standard.
3. **Systematize:** define message hierarchy, vocabulary, visual/motion behaviors, accessibility constraints, and reusable content patterns.
4. **Apply:** make only additive, reversible changes consistent with the existing product language unless an audited defect warrants a redesign.
5. **Audit:** challenge every artifact for clarity, distinctiveness, truthfulness, accessibility, cohesion, and customer fit. Link evidence and reject surface novelty.

## Creative quality gate

Score each applicable dimension from 1–10 with direct evidence: strategic clarity, distinctiveness, customer relevance, product truth, visual cohesion, interaction/motion purpose, accessibility, readability, adaptability, and legal/claim safety. A customer-facing stage is no-go below 9 on any critical dimension or if its claims lack substantiation.

## Authority

Agents can analyze, propose, and create internal drafts. Require approval before publishing, changing a production interface, using protected assets, or making external claims.

## Handoff

Write the current brand thesis, evidence, unresolved tensions, approved rules, rejected directions, artifact locations, score, and exact next safe action into the shared ledger.
