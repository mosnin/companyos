DESIGN THINKING DOCTRINE — SECTION 07: Service Design and Experience Excellence

Purpose
Build a repeatable, professional-grade design thinking system that converts ambiguous user reality into validated solutions.
This is a knowledge and execution reference. It focuses on method, structure, and artifacts.

Core Outputs
1) Clear artifacts (canvases, maps, hypotheses, tests)
2) Evidence (observations, measurements, decisions)
3) Iteration loop (learn → update → retest)

Operating Rules
1) User truth beats team opinion
2) Behavior beats self-report when they conflict
3) Fast loops beat perfect plans
4) Evidence updates the model, not ego
5) Make uncertainty explicit, then attack it

============================================================
Scope
Design end-to-end experiences by aligning UI, UX, experience design, service design, and value design.

A. Layers of Design
1) UI: screens and controls
2) UX: flow and cognitive load
3) Experience: journey and emotions
4) Service: backstage processes
5) Value: ecosystem outcomes and co-creation

B. Excellence Progression
1) Operational excellence: reliability and efficiency
2) Service excellence: consistent end-to-end quality
3) Value excellence: co-creation and ecosystem fit

C. Journey Map
Include:
1) Stages: discover, evaluate, onboard, use, support, renew
2) Touchpoints
3) Emotions and expectations
4) Pain points and moments of truth
5) Opportunities

D. Service Blueprint
Include:
1) Customer actions
2) Frontstage interactions
3) Backstage actions
4) Support processes
5) Evidence and artifacts
6) Failure points and recovery actions

Deliverables
1) Journey map
2) Service blueprint
3) Experience metrics
4) Improvement backlog
