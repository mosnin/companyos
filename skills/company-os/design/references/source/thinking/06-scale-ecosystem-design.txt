DESIGN THINKING DOCTRINE — SECTION 06: Scale and Business Ecosystem Design

Purpose
Build a repeatable, professional-grade design thinking system that converts ambiguous user reality into validated solutions.
This is a knowledge and execution reference. It focuses on method, structure, and artifacts.

Core Outputs
1) Clear artifacts (canvases, maps, hypotheses, tests)
2) Evidence (observations, measurements, decisions)
3) Iteration loop (learn → update → retest)

Operating Rules
1) User truth beats team opinion
2) Behavior beats self-report when they conflict
3) Fast loops beat perfect plans
4) Evidence updates the model, not ego
5) Make uncertainty explicit, then attack it

============================================================
Scope
Move from validated solution to a system that grows without breaking.

A. Ecosystem Mapping
Actors
• Customers, users, partners, suppliers, regulators, internal teams, platforms

Map
1) Roles and responsibilities
2) Value exchanged
3) Dependencies and bottlenecks
4) Incentives and conflicts
5) Failure points under scale

B. Scaling Preconditions
1) Repeatable value delivery
2) Stable operations and quality controls
3) Predictable unit economics
4) Clear handoffs and ownership
5) Monitoring and feedback loops

C. Operating Model
Define:
1) Acquire, onboard, deliver, support, renew
2) Decision rights and escalation paths
3) Quality standards and SLAs
4) Tooling and data systems
5) Hiring and training needs

D. Growth Mechanisms
1) Acquisition loops
2) Retention loops
3) Partner distribution
4) Expansion revenue
5) Network effects (when real)

Deliverables
1) Ecosystem map
2) Operating model blueprint
3) Scale risks list
4) Monitoring dashboard

End of file.
