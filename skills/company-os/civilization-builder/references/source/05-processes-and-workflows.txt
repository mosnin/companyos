CIVILIZATION BUILDER ARCHITECT — SECTION 05: Processes and Workflows

Purpose
Translate principles into a repeatable execution method for diagnosing and designing builder grade systems.

Master workflow: Civilization Builder Design Process

Inputs
- Problem domain
- Current constraints
- Population or market affected
- Available capital, talent, and time horizon
- Existing infrastructure and institutional context

Step 1 Define the target system
Output
- One sentence defining the system to be built
Template
We are building a system that enables [population] to do [function] with lower [cost, time, risk, friction] by changing the [infrastructure, production, governance, platform] layer.

Step 2 Map the stack
For each of the five civilization layers, answer:
- What exists now
- What is missing
- What is the bottleneck
- What dependencies matter most

Step 3 Identify the binding bottleneck
Choose one bottleneck only.
Examples
- energy availability
- logistics capacity
- compute cost
- regulatory legibility
- manufacturing throughput

Step 4 Choose the builder mode
Modes
- state builder
- infrastructure builder
- industrial builder
- platform builder
- ecosystem builder

Step 5 Design the enabling substrate
Questions
- What foundation must exist first
- What standard or protocol matters
- What control points are necessary
- What can be vertically integrated now

Step 6 Define the scale path
Stages
1 pilot
2 reliability
3 standardization
4 throughput expansion
5 ecosystem expansion
6 governance and maintenance

Step 7 Instrument the system
Metrics
- adoption rate
- throughput
- unit cost decline
- reliability or uptime
- complement creation
- switching cost or retention
- administrative legibility

Step 8 Define failure modes
Examples
- overcomplexity before product market fit
- dependency fragility
- fragmented interfaces
- capital starvation before scale efficiencies
- regulatory non fit

Outputs checklist
- system definition
- stack map
- bottleneck statement
- architecture choice
- scale path
- metrics dashboard
- risk register
