# Reality Levels

- R0: research, plans, schemas, contracts, reports, or designs only.
- R1: exact product primitives exist.
- R2: the relevant capability runs in isolation.
- R3: the First Reality journey runs end to end.
- R4: the final required capability set is connected, checkpointed, and usable by a fresh user.
- R5: current artifact and runtime evidence passes independent reality acceptance.

Higher levels cannot float above missing lower levels. Documentation volume has no effect on Reality Level.
