# Director Integration Points

The durable director must invoke Mission Execution Control:

1. at mission start;
2. before discovery and reality-spike dispatch;
3. before every manager or worker fabric is returned;
4. after artifact or runtime evidence;
5. after failure, retry, and rework;
6. before evaluator construction or calibration;
7. before scheduled wake execution;
8. before organizational expansion;
9. before packaging and finalization.

The director persists the governor decision and embeds its digest, allowed work classes, current bottleneck, deadline, and replacement orders into every execution fabric. The organization compiler rejects a stale or bypassed decision.
