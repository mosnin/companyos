# Accept Outcome Reality

The final judge must evaluate reality, not the team's story about reality.

Acceptance binds the original objective and required outcome claims to actual observations and
independent evaluator receipts. Production completion summaries may be retained for audit, but
they are not admissible evidence for the release decision.

Every required claim must pass independently. Missing evidence, stale objective bindings,
self evaluation, or a failed evaluator calibration blocks acceptance.
