# Innovation Bets

Use innovation to create what customers cannot yet articulate. Treat evidence as a way to refine a bold thesis, not a demand for backward-looking proof.

## Bet contract

- Point of view: the non-obvious future or experience this bet makes possible.
- Hypothesis and the belief being tested.
- Customer, strategic, or technical upside.
- Existing evidence and why it is insufficient.
- Smallest credible experiment or prototype.
- Learning metric, time/cost cap, risk boundary, and owner.
- Scale, pivot, hold, and kill thresholds.
- Required approvals for external/customer/production exposure.

Favor bets with a clear, differentiated product thesis and a plausible path to an experience people will love, even when direct demand data is absent. Run the fastest test that can change the decision without reducing the vision to a shallow feature poll. Preserve negative results. Promote only a validated or strategically reaffirmed bet into the portfolio; otherwise stop or archive with learning.
