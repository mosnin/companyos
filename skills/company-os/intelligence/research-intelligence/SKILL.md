# Research Intelligence

Use this as the intelligence layer for the Company OS. It produces decision-ready evidence; it does not fabricate certainty or outsource judgment to search results.

## Research modes

| Need | Skill | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broad decision question | `$evidence-research-campaign` | Claim-grounded research report with counterevidence. |
| Real user signal | `$voice-of-customer` | Pain map, verbatim themes, segments, and opportunity evidence. |
| Implementation options | `$technology-radar` | Feasibility and adoption decision for technology choices. |
| Evidence is thin but opportunity matters | `$innovation-bets` | Explicit hypothesis, smallest experiment, learning metric, and kill criteria. |
| Control plane | `$company-scorecard` | A decision dashboard that exposes outcomes, confidence, health, and exceptions. |

## Decision rule

Be **vision-led and evidence-informed**. Use data to understand reality, sharpen choices, and expose risk; do not require historical demand to authorize a genuinely new category or experience.

Choose a **data-confirmed** decision when relevant, fresh, independent evidence already establishes demand or causality. Choose a **vision-led innovation bet** when a coherent insight about the future, human behavior, technology, or experience is stronger than the available historical data. State the insight plainly, identify what would make it wrong, cap the initial investment, and learn quickly. Do not disguise preference as data or call novelty a strategy without a distinct thesis.

## Research contract

Before searching, define decision, scope, audience, cutoff date, affected users, evidence standard, budget, counterevidence lane, privacy constraints, and stop rule. Preserve sources and timestamps. Separate observed fact, source claim, inference, recommendation, and unknown.

## Handoff

Send approved conclusions to Strategy Pillar as a decision memo. Convert accepted recommendations into a portfolio bet or project only after the evidence, confidence, and risk are recorded.

When the next step is a testable belief, bind a
`company-os.scientific-method-packet.v1` using the Company OS scientific-method
playbook. Research remains a sensor and does not own completion.
