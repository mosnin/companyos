# Voice of Customer

Find what people actually struggle to do, not merely what they say they would buy.

## Sources

Use consented first-party feedback where authorized, plus relevant public discussions, reviews, communities, interviews, support themes, and practitioner content. Record source context, date, segment, and sampling limitations. Never collect private data, bypass access controls, or identify individuals unnecessarily.

## Analysis

Extract job-to-be-done, trigger, current workaround, pain severity, frequency, desired outcome, switching friction, willingness signal, and exact language. Segment by role, context, maturity, and urgency. Seek disconfirming segments and non-users.

## Output

Produce a pain/opportunity map with evidence density and confidence. Keep quotes short and attributable. Distinguish widespread demand from vivid anecdotes. Hand validated opportunities to Portfolio Direction; hand language and experience implications to the future brand/content system.
