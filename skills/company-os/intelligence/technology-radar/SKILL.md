# Technology Radar

Evaluate a technology against the product and operating model—not novelty alone.

## Radar categories

Classify each candidate as **adopt**, **trial**, **assess**, **hold**, or **retire**.

Evaluate capability fit, maturity, primary documentation, ecosystem, integration effort, security/privacy, reliability, latency, cost, lock-in, operating burden, migration/rollback, team fit, and customer differentiation. Prefer primary sources and small feasibility tests for high-impact decisions.

## Output

Write a technical decision record with problem, alternatives, evidence, architecture fit, quantified assumptions, risks, adoption path, experiment, success/abort criteria, and reconsideration date. Do not equate vendor availability with production readiness.
