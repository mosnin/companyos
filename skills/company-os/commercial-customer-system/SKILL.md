# Commercial and Customer System

Operate commercial work as a learning system, not an activity factory.

## Core loop

1. Define a target customer, job, acute pain, desired outcome, and trust boundary.
2. Gather first-party and public evidence ethically; label sample size, recency, selection bias, and confidence.
3. Turn evidence into a testable value hypothesis, adoption metric, retention/health signal, and disconfirming condition.
4. Route approved product, service, and content work into the delivery system; do not send, sell, or alter customer data without approval.
5. Review exceptions: churn risk, stalled adoption, sales-cycle friction, recurring support themes, promise/proof gaps, and cost-to-serve.

## Decision model

Use evidence where it exists. When evidence cannot exist because a proposal is novel, log a bounded innovation bet with a clear customer promise, a low-risk test, learning metric, time/cost cap, and pivot/kill rule. Do not disguise intuition as data.

## Scorecard contract

For every signal record source, owner, baseline, target, freshness, confidence, decision threshold, and linked evidence. Useful panels include activation, time-to-value, engagement, retention/health, feedback themes, pipeline quality, conversion, service burden, and trust incidents. Missing data is an explicit gap, never green.

## Authority and safety

Agents may research, segment, summarize, draft, and propose. Approval is required for customer contact, pricing or contractual claims, campaigns, CRM mutation, exports, payment actions, or any use of sensitive personal data.

## Handoff

Persist decisions, confidence, evidence, experiment status, approval state, risks, and exact next action in the Company OS ledger. Escalate meaningful customer harm or privacy risk immediately.

When the work is offer architecture, value validation, or reliable delivery of a promised outcome, load `$value-creation-delivery` as the value-creation and delivery overlay.
Do not send `$value-creation-delivery` to Luna workers.
