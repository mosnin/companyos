VALUE CREATION + VALUE DELIVERY DOCTRINE — SECTION 01: Business Model Core and Definitions

Purpose
Turn the provided context into an execution-ready system for designing offers (value creation)
and delivering outcomes reliably at scale (value delivery).

Inclusion rule
All user-provided bullets are included. Additions are only connective tissue, templates, and
operational definitions to make execution possible.

============================================================

Included context (kept)
• A business is a repeatable process that (1. Creates and delivers something of value, 2. That other people want or need, 3. At a price they’re willing to pay, 4. In a way that satisfies the customers needs and expectations, 5. So the business brings in enough profit to make it worthwhile)
• At the core every business is composed of 5 independent parts (value creation, marketing, sales, value delivery, finance)

Operational definitions (added)
• Value: measurable improvement in a customer’s state relative to cost, risk, and effort.
• Offer: packaged promise of value including scope, price, terms, and proof.
• Delivery: production system that turns inputs into outcomes.

The 5-part model (handoff clarity)
1) Value creation: decide what to build and why anyone cares.
2) Marketing: create attention and demand.
3) Sales: convert demand into commitments with terms.
4) Value delivery: reliably produce outcomes.
5) Finance: unit economics and cash flow.

Core artifacts
• One-page business system map
• Handoff contracts between creation ↔ marketing ↔ sales ↔ delivery ↔ finance

4) Delivery workflow summary
