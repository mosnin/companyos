Shared operating rules
- Value is a measurable improvement in a customer's state relative to cost,
  risk, and effort.
- Creation decides what and why. Delivery produces the outcome reliably.
- Promise only what delivery can do. Satisfaction is reality minus
  expectations.
- Do not invent customers, willingness to pay, CAC, or delivery capacity.
