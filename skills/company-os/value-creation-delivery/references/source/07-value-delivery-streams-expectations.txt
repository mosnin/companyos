Value stream (kept)
End-to-end steps producing outcomes. Define steps, handoffs, cycle time, failure modes, acceptance criteria.

Distribution channel (kept)
How value reaches the customer. Must match trust, effort, support intensity, context.

Expectation effect (kept)
Satisfaction = reality minus expectations.
Rules: promise only what you can deliver reliably; make progress visible; communicate constraints early.

Predictability (kept)
Predictability is a feature: consistent timelines and outcomes.

Quality and quality signals (kept)
Quality meets acceptance criteria. Quality signals reduce pre-purchase uncertainty.

Throughout (interpreted as throughput, kept term)
Throughput: completed outcomes per unit time.
