Scaling ladder (kept terms, clarified)
Duplication: repeat delivery unit consistently.
Multiplication: add parallel capacity.
Scale: increase output while maintaining or improving unit economics and quality.

Accumulation (kept)
Build persistent assets: SOPs, templates, automation, proof library, training, proprietary data.

Amplification and force multipliers (kept)
Increase output per input via tooling, automation, partnerships, productization, instrumentation.

Systemization (kept)
Standardized workflow, QA gates, documentation, onboarding, measurement.

Barrier to competition (kept)
Switching costs, trust, integrated workflows, learning curve advantage.

Template: Scale readiness checklist
Stable acceptance criteria, predictable cycle time, low defects, known capacity constraints, documented SOPs, measurable unit economics.
