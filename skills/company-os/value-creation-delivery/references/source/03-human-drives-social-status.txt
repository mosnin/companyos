Included context (kept)
• Core human drives (drive to acquire, the drive to bond, the drive to learn, the drive to defend, the drive to feel)
• Social status

Drive mapping
Map the offer to 1–3 primary drives:
• acquire: gain resources, save money, increase income
• bond: belonging, connection, trust
• learn: competence, clarity, mastery
• defend: reduce risk, avoid loss, safety
• feel: pleasure, identity, meaning

Status mechanics
Status changes willingness to pay, urgency, retention, referrals.
Rule: status claims require respected signals.
