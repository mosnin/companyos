Perceived value (kept, clarified)
Increases with outcome clarity, proof, reduced risk, reduced effort, and status or identity benefits.

Modularity (kept)
Use modules to reduce delivery cost, increase pricing flexibility, speed iteration.

Bundling and unbundling (kept)
Bundle to simplify and increase total value; unbundle to reduce friction and match segment needs.

Intermediation and disintermediation (kept)
Intermediation reduces friction; disintermediation reduces cost and increases speed/control.

Alternatives and trade-offs (kept)
Compete against direct competitors, substitutes, doing nothing. Make trade-offs explicit.
