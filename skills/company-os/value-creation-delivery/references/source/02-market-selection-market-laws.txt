The iron law of the market (operational)
Markets pay for outcomes that are urgent, valuable, credible, de-risked, and repeatable.

Market scorecard (10 factors)
Score 1–5 each:
1) urgency
2) market size
3) pricing potential
4) cost of customer acquisition
5) cost of value delivery
6) uniqueness of offer
7) speed to market
8) up-front investment
9) upsell potential
10) evergreen potential

Hidden benefits of competition (kept)
• Proof demand exists
• Benchmarks for pricing and performance
• Channel validation
Use it to identify: complaints, pricing gaps, slow delivery, unreliability.

Mercenary vs crusader rule (kept, clarified)
• Mercenary: optimize for profit and clarity of demand.
• Crusader: optimize for mission and meaning.
Operating rule (added): pick one as primary; keep the other as constraints.
