Critical assumptions (kept)
Demand at price, deliverability, CAC, margins under real support load.

Prototype (kept)
Minimum artifact to test one critical assumption.

Shadow testing (kept)
Test demand without full build: preorder, paid pilot, priced waitlist, sales calls.

Minimum viable offer (kept)
Smallest sellable offer that delivers a real outcome and generates learning.

Iteration cycle (kept)
hypothesize → smallest test → measure → learn → revise

Iteration velocity (kept)
Increase loops per unit time by narrowing tests, modularity, and clear metrics.

Feedback (kept)
Prefer behavioral feedback plus short “why” interviews.

Incremental augmentation and field testing (kept)
Augment after baseline delivery is reliable; test in real conditions.
