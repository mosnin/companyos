Fayol, Koontz, or any named MBA school. Load one primary file for the named
decision. Do not invent a fourth executable hop.

Shared operating rules
- Management turns potential into performance. Resources without a named
  owner, plan, and check are not an accepted outcome.
- Functions form a cycle: plan, organize, staff, direct, coordinate, control.
- Executable delegation stays master → manager → worker.
- Low-level management is a narrower Sol-manager charter, not a new runtime
  role and not a parent hop. Middle and low-level are peer Sol managers.
  Staff are Luna workers.
- Do not invent headcount, authority, or a second orchestrator.
- Tokens follow the global bottleneck, not the org chart. Do not spawn an
  audit manager or paste this pack into prompts. `$govern-outcome-execution`
  still wins for spend.
- Control scores outputs. `$execute-outcome-evaluator` and
  `$run-outcome-loop` still win. A manager score is not a score.
- A score is correct only after `$calibrate-outcome-evaluator` strictly
  ranks known-quality candidates and the executor is not a producer.
- `$force-first-execution` still wins for motion. Plans and audits are
  not progress.
