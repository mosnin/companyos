CORPORATE MANAGEMENT — SECTION 03: Levels of Management

Purpose
Name four corporate tiers without adding a fabric role.

Tiers
1) Top / senior management
   Owns goals, policy, and long-horizon decisions for the whole company or
   program. Main role: planning and decision making.
2) Middle management
   Implements senior plans, coordinates departments, and is the bridge
   between senior decisions and daily work. Main role: coordinating and
   implementing.
3) Low-level management
   Directs one team on one bounded outcome. Supervises daily work and local
   control. Main role: supervising and executing the slice.
4) Employees / staff
   Perform the named work packet. They do not spawn children, set strategy,
   or accept their own work.

Rules
- A person or agent has one tier per charter.
- Senior is never a Luna worker.
- Staff never act as master.
- Do not invent a fifth tier to fit a preferred org chart.
