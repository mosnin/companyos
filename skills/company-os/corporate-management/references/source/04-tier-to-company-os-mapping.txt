CORPORATE MANAGEMENT — SECTION 04: Tier to Company OS Mapping

Purpose
Map the four corporate tiers onto the existing three-hop authority model.

Hard cap
Executable delegation is master → manager → worker. The overlay may name
four corporate tiers. It may not add a fourth executable hop, a new
orchestrator, or a scheduler/runtime enablement.

Mapping
- Top / senior management = Company OS master.
  Uses `$company-os`, `$govern-outcome-execution`, `$mission-execution-control`,
  and `$navigation-control`. Versions the Program Contract. Decides charter,
  design, verification, and integration barriers. Names the global bottleneck.
- Middle management = Sol manager of a department or program outcome.
  Uses `$manage-company-program`. Coordinates interfaces, implements senior
  plans, and reports at phase barriers. May not change strategy, roadmap
  ownership, or authority.
- Low-level management = Sol manager of one slice or worker team.
  Same fabric role as middle management. Narrower charter: direct the team,
  control local evidence, escalate blockers. Not a new runtime role and
  not a parent under middle management.
- Employees / staff = Luna workers.
  Use `$execute-bounded-task`. One packet, one write boundary, one receipt.
  Worker completion is never acceptance.

Rules
- Middle and low-level are peer Sol managers. Both report to master.
  Wider vs narrower charter is not a parent hop.
- Do not spawn a manager to sit between master and manager.
- Do not spawn a manager under a manager.
- Do not spawn a worker to act as low-level management.
- Four names do not require four agent layers. A bounded pilot may have
  two peer managers.
- Derive manager count from independently accountable outcomes, not a
  fixed 1:1:1:N headcount slogan.
