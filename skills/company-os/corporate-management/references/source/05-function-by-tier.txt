CORPORATE MANAGEMENT — SECTION 05: Function by Tier

Purpose
Assign the management cycle without letting every tier do every function.

Senior / master
- Primary: planning and decision making.
- Also: organizing the program contract and controlling at barriers.
- Owns the global bottleneck and which resources follow it.
- Does not: perform staff labor or rewrite worker packets after dispatch
  except to stop, replace, or re-scope.

Middle / department or program Sol manager
- Primary: organizing, coordinating, and implementing.
- Also: staffing its Luna team and controlling slice evidence before
  reporting upward.
- Reports a local view of the bottleneck. Does not redirect program
  resources away from the master's named bottleneck.
- Does not: set company strategy, command a peer manager, or accept its
  own integration.

Low-level / slice Sol manager
- Primary: directing and local controlling.
- Also: staffing the next packet when the charter already allows it.
- Same reporting destination as middle: master. Not a child of middle.
- Does not: open a new department, change the program contract, skip
  independent review, or perform staff labor.

Staff / Luna worker
- Primary: execute the named packet.
- Does not: plan the company, organize other workers, or control acceptance.

Cycle rule
Every accepted outcome still needs the full cycle. The missing function is
the management bottleneck. Do not add a meeting, extra manager, or LLM
audit loop to hide a missing function. Control is verified evaluator
receipts or controller `score-quality` bound to artifact bytes. A manager
score is not a score. Audits are sensors; they are not accepted outcomes.
