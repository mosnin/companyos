CORPORATE MANAGEMENT — SECTION 02: Functions of Management

Purpose
Name the management cycle before dispatching work.

Functions
1) Planning: decide in advance what, how, when, and who. Sets direction and
   reduces uncertainty. First in the cycle.
2) Organizing: arrange work, resources, and authority. Creates structure and
   coordinates activities.
3) Staffing: place the right actor on the right job. In Company OS this is
   charter and packet assignment, not HRIS mutation.
4) Directing: guide, supervise, and lead the assigned actors to the named
   outcome.
5) Coordinating: integrate efforts so lanes do not duplicate or collide.
6) Controlling: measure actual performance against the standard and take
   corrective action. Last in the cycle, first in the next. In Company OS
   this is a calibrated evaluator receipt or controller `score-quality`
   bound to artifact bytes, not a manager-written score.

Rules
- Skip control and the plan is decoration.
- A manager score, audit, or production narrative is not a score.
- An uncalibrated number is not a correct score. The judge must strictly
  rank known-quality candidates; the executor must not be a producer.
- Skip first artifact, runnable candidate, and observed behavior and the
  score has nothing real to judge.
- Skip staffing and the right work goes to the wrong actor.
- Skip coordinating and two lanes write the same boundary.
- Do not treat the cycle as a new Company OS execution loop.
- Company OS barriers remain charter, design, verification, and
  integration. The cycle names functions inside those barriers.

End of file.
