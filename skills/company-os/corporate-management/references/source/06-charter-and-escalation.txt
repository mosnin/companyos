CORPORATE MANAGEMENT — SECTION 06: Charter and Escalation

Purpose
Make the tier and the owned functions visible on the charter before work
starts.

Name the tier and owned functions in compact mission-charter or work-packet
text. Fill the existing `reporting_destination` field. Do not add a
management_tier schema field, preflight capability, or fabric role.
- management tier: senior | middle | low_level | staff
- owned functions: the subset of the cycle this actor may perform
- reporting destination: master for every manager; parent manager for staff
- escalation: the first blocker that leaves this tier

Escalation
- Staff stop and report to the parent manager.
- Every Sol manager escalates strategy, authority, budget, barrier
  decisions, and the global bottleneck to master.
- A peer manager may be notified of a cross-lane collision. Notification
  is not command and not a parent hop.
- Senior / master does not escalate to a manager. It decides, stops, or
  asks the operator.

Safety
- Do not contact customers, hire, spend, or mutate HRIS from this overlay.
- Do not enable the Company OS scheduler or runtime from this overlay.
- Rejected commands must not mutate governed state.
