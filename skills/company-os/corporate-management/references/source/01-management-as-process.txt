CORPORATE MANAGEMENT — SECTION 01: Management as a Process

Purpose
Treat management as the continuous process that turns potential into
performance. Ideas, money, machines, materials, and people are valuable only
when combined with a named owner, a plan, and a check.

Operating definition
Management is a series of continuous, interrelated functions performed to
achieve organizational goals efficiently and effectively. The functions are
not job titles. They are the cycle every governed outcome must pass through.

Inclusion
- Planning, organizing, staffing, directing, coordinating, and controlling
  are one cycle.
- Decision making and change sit inside the cycle; they are not a seventh
  execution loop.
- Anything minus management is zero: unowned resources do not become
  accepted Company OS outcomes.

Company OS rule
This overlay does not own authority, leases, fabric, or completion.
`$manage-company-program` still wins. Rejected commands must not mutate
governed state.
