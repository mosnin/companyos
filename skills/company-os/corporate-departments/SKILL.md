# Corporate Departments

This is a thinking overlay for how Company OS departments think. It does not own authority, leases, fabric, spend, or completion. `$manage-company-program`, `$company-blueprint`, `$department-charters`, `$corporate-management`, `$govern-outcome-execution`, and `$force-first-execution` still win.

This doctrine is an original compiled operating template stored with its source pack. It is not a claim of personal identity, private knowledge, quoted speech, or affiliation with McKinsey, BCG, Bain, or any named firm.

Do not send this skill to Luna workers. Do not use it as the master persona. Do not attach it to a manager whose outcome is not a named department function.

## Every heartbeat

Before directing a department, answer these:

1. Name the preset department. Do not invent a parallel team to satisfy a slogan.
2. Name the governing question. Structure it as an issue tree whose branches do not overlap and together cover the decision.
3. Name the economic object: customer, cash, capacity, capability, or risk. Activity is not the object.
4. Name the evidence that would change the recommendation. If none exists, gather the smallest fact packet first.
5. Pause if this is a fourth hop, a worker setting department strategy, a claim without a source, or an unapproved customer, financial, legal, or hiring write.

Do not hire, spend, mutate HRIS or CRM, publish, or enable the scheduler or runtime from this overlay.

## Forced moves

- Preset packs are Strategy, Program Management, Product, Engineering, Brand, Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, Finance, Operations, Human Resources, and Security/Legal.
- Marketing creates demand and meaning. Sales converts qualified demand into revenue. Do not merge their decision rights. Marketing execution uses `$marketing-os`.
- Operations keeps flow reliable. Finance keeps cash and allocation true. People acquire and align capability.
- Issue trees before workplans. Mutually exclusive branches. Collectively exhaustive coverage. Hypothesis and kill rule before analysis.
- Unit economics before scale. Pipeline is not revenue. Brand is not a campaign. Headcount is not a capability.
- Tokens follow the global bottleneck, not the org chart. Do not paste this pack, `$company-os`, or `$elastic-company-os` into a manager or worker prompt.
- Do not spawn an audit or review manager. Control scores outputs through `$execute-outcome-evaluator` and `$run-outcome-loop`. A manager score is not a score.
- `$force-first-execution` still wins. A framework memo without a candidate is not progress.
- Any manager escalates to master. A peer notice is not command.
