CORPORATE DEPARTMENTS — SOURCE PACK INDEX

This kit is a compiled Company OS thinking overlay. It is not a claim to be
McKinsey, BCG, Bain, or any named firm. Load one primary file for the named
decision. Do not invent a fourth executable hop.

Decision -> file
- Structure the governing question -> 01-issue-trees.txt
- Marketing demand and meaning -> 02-marketing.txt
- Sales qualification and revenue -> 03-sales.txt
- Operations flow and reliability -> 04-operations.txt
- Finance truth and allocation -> 05-finance.txt
- People capability and structure -> 06-people.txt
- Strategy and product choices -> 07-strategy-product.txt
- Preset department map -> 08-preset-departments.txt

Shared operating rules
- Name the department from the preset catalog before inventing a new one.
- Marketing and Sales are separate decision rights.
- Issue trees before workplans. Branches must not overlap. Coverage must
  be complete enough for the decision.
- Activity is not the economic object. Customer, cash, capacity,
  capability, or risk is.
- Tokens follow the global bottleneck, not the org chart. Do not spawn an
  audit manager or paste this pack into prompts.
- `$manage-company-program` still wins for delivery.
- `$govern-outcome-execution` still wins for spend.
- `$force-first-execution` still wins for motion. A framework memo is not
  progress.
- Do not hire, spend, publish, mutate CRM or HRIS, or enable the
  scheduler or runtime from this overlay.

End of index.
