CORPORATE DEPARTMENTS — SECTION 08: Preset Department Map

Purpose
Name the reusable corporate packs. Compile with `$company-blueprint`.
Charter with `$department-charters`. Staff tiers with
`$corporate-management`. Store created agents as department slots.

Preset packs
- executive-strategy: direction, portfolio, scorecard.
  Doctrine: 07-strategy-product.txt
- program-management: roadmaps, dependencies, decision records.
  Doctrine: 01-issue-trees.txt
- product: customer research, product strategy, accepted slices.
  Doctrine: 07-strategy-product.txt
- engineering-quality: build, verify, block unsafe release.
  Doctrine: 01-issue-trees.txt
- brand-creative: brand system, creative, content.
  Doctrine: 02-marketing.txt for demand meaning; brand skill still wins
  for craft.
- marketing: demand generation, campaigns, market research.
  Doctrine: 02-marketing.txt
- sales: qualification, pipeline, revenue conversion.
  Doctrine: 03-sales.txt
- customer-success: value realized, retention, voice of customer.
  Doctrine: 03-sales.txt for handoff; commercial skill still wins.
- finance: truth, forecast, allocation.
  Doctrine: 05-finance.txt
- operations: flow, incidents, capacity.
  Doctrine: 04-operations.txt
- human-resources: roles, hiring plans, org slices.
  Doctrine: 06-people.txt
- security-compliance: security, privacy, legal review.
  Doctrine: 01-issue-trees.txt for risk structure.

Rules
- Marketing and Sales are separate packs. Do not restore a combined
  commercial-growth department to save a slot.
- Archetypes select subsets. software-company includes the full
  corporate set. agency stays leaner. professional-services stays lean
  and auto-selects Sales for revenue-growth.
- Slots are templates, not running threads. Senior / master is never a
  department slot.
- A department exists only when mission, capabilities, playbooks,
  decision rights, interfaces, metrics, approvals, and shutdown path
  are concrete.
- Do not enable the scheduler or runtime from this overlay.

End of file.
