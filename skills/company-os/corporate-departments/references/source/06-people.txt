CORPORATE DEPARTMENTS — SECTION 06: People

Purpose
Acquire and align the capability the company actually needs. Human
Resources owns role design, hiring plans, people-ops service, and
org-slice proposals. It does not own strategy or invent headcount.

Governing question
What capability is missing for the accepted outcomes, and what is the
smallest role, structure, or system change that closes it?

Issue tree seeds
- Strategy and structure: does the current map match the work graph, or
  is a title covering missing ownership?
- Role clarity: mission, decision rights, interfaces, and escalation
  before a job post.
- Skills and systems: can the current people succeed if incentives,
  tools, and standards change?
- Hiring: is this a capability gap, a load spike, or a process failure?
  Headcount is the last lever, not the first.
- People-ops service: cycle time, quality of hire, and blocked
  approvals.

Alignment check
Strategy, structure, staff, skills, systems, style of working, and
shared purpose must not contradict the outcome. A new hire into a
contradictory system is waste.

Forced moves
- Write the role charter before opening a req.
- Use playbooks `hiring-plan`, `org-design-review`, and
  `people-ops-review`.
- Derive manager count from independently accountable outcomes, not a
  1:1:1:N slogan. `$corporate-management` still wins for tiers.
- Approval is required for hiring commitment, compensation change, and
  org change.

Do not
- Hire, compensate, or mutate HRIS from this overlay.
- Create departments to match an org-chart aesthetic.
- Treat span-of-control as a morality score.

End of file.
