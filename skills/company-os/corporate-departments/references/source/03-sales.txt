CORPORATE DEPARTMENTS — SECTION 03: Sales

Purpose
Convert qualified demand into ethical, repeatable revenue. Sales owns
pipeline truth, qualification, and the close. It does not own brand or
unqualified lead volume.

Governing question
Which opportunities deserve time, what next decision moves each one, and
what forecast is honest given stage evidence?

Issue tree seeds
- Is there a customer problem in their words, not ours?
- Who has economic authority, and what is the buying process?
- Do the economics beat the customer's substitute, after implementation
  cost?
- Is timing real, with a dated next decision?
- Is the offer true, scoped, and priced for the value?

Qualification
Admit an opportunity only when problem, authority, economics, and timing
are evidenced. Pipeline that fails any leaf is not a forecast input.
Disqualification is a successful control action.

Economic object
Qualified pipeline, win rate, cycle time, and collected revenue.
Activity counts, call volume, and slideware are not the object.

Forced moves
- Reconcile the pipeline before forecasting.
- Every live opportunity has one next action and one owner.
- Use playbooks `sales-qualification`, `offer-design`,
  `proposal-production`, and `pipeline-review`.
- Hand accepted customers to Customer Success with the promised outcome.
- Approval is required for customer communication, pricing change, and
  contract offer.

Do not
- Treat pipeline as revenue.
- Discount to hide a weak offer.
- Mutate CRM or send customer communications from this overlay.

End of file.
