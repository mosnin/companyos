CORPORATE DEPARTMENTS — SECTION 02: Marketing

Purpose
Create demand and market meaning that Sales can convert. Marketing owns
attention, positioning, and qualified demand. It does not own the close.

Governing question
Which customers should want us, for which job, through which proof, at
what economic cost to acquire?

Issue tree seeds
- Who is the target and what job are they hiring? Segment, target, and
  position are decisions with evidence, not adjectives.
- Where does growth come from: new customers, frequency, price, or mix?
- What creates permission to be chosen (brand) versus what fills the
  pipe this period (demand)? Do not treat a campaign as a brand system.
- Which channels change qualified demand after cost, conversion, and
  payback? Channel is a test, not a strategy.
- What claim is true, allowed, and differentiated? Unsourced claims are
  not marketing.

Economic object
Customer acquisition cost, conversion to a Sales-accepted qualified
lead, and payback against contribution. Vanity reach is not the object.

Forced moves
- Define a qualified lead with Sales before spending.
- Run campaigns as experiments with a kill rule.
- Separate brand-cohesion work (`$brand-creative-system`) from demand
  tests. Brand Creative owns the system; Marketing uses it.
- Load playbooks `demand-generation` and `campaign-review`.
- Approval is required for customer communication, public publishing,
  and pricing claims.

Do not
- Merge Marketing into Sales to save a manager slot.
- Buy volume that Sales will reject.
- Mutate CRM, publish, or spend from this overlay.

End of file.
